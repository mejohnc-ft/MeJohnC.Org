## Work
I moved into provisioning after starting in frontline support. The work was highly manual: preparing machines, tracking inventory, managing handoffs, and checking that equipment was ready for the people receiving it.

### Recover the backlog

I cleared an inherited 72-ticket COVID-era backlog within six months. Alongside the queue, I rebuilt SOPs, service-level expectations, inventory tracking, knowledge, and media-sanitization controls.

### Learn the whole process

Doing the work manually exposed the failure points: missing information, configuration problems, inventory movement, and technician handoffs. That operating experience became the basis for the process changes that followed.
