## Work
I built out deployment and configuration automation on Immy.Bot while continuing to own the provisioning process.

### One delivery chain

Field Support Engineer and Provisioning Lead were concurrent responsibilities. I served as the primary logistics coordinator connecting equipment, configuration, inventory, and onsite engineer readiness.

### Less hands-on repetition

Across the provisioning improvements, active technician effort on a fresh-machine onboarding path fell from roughly 45 minutes to about two minutes. That measures hands-on effort, not total elapsed delivery time. Human quality control remained part of the workflow.

Automation carried more of the repeatable work while I continued managing the process around it.

### September · iPhone 16 Pro

I bought into the iPhone 16 Pro generation and could barely tell the difference. The chip interested the hardware addict in me, but the everyday experience didn’t justify the change. I should have kept the money.
