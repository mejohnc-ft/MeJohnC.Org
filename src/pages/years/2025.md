## Work
I handed day-to-day provisioning to a new owner after improving its efficiency and margin, and moved into AI Automation Engineering full-time.

### A system someone else could run

The handoff carried forward the operating process, documentation, controls, and automation. The work could continue under a new owner.

That is what I’m proud of: taking a manual operation through recovery, process improvement, and automation, then leaving a working system for the next person.

### Taking the experience with me

I began applying that experience across service and client teams: learning how people work, identifying the constraints, building with them, and following through on adoption.

### September–October · iPhone 17 Pro, watches, and more

The iPhone 17 Pro felt like a worthwhile upgrade to me. Cooling, memory, charging, and the display gave me more to appreciate than the previous change had.

Looking back across all these phones, I can imagine a much simpler path: keep the iPhone X, move to the 13 Pro Max, then the 17 Pro. Those are the moments that stand out most strongly in my own experience.

I love hardware, but being excited by it doesn’t make every upgrade meaningful. Some of my favorite devices were the ones I should have kept longer.
