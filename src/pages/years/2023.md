## Work
After roughly 18 months of hands-on provisioning, I had enough context to redesign the process end to end. I standardized the knowledge, controls, and handoffs while continuing to steward the department.

### Processes people could follow

The work was about more than a faster configuration step. It meant making the operating process repeatable: clear instructions, dependable inventory tracking, consistent checks, and handoffs that other people could follow.

### Quality First Award

I received centrexIT’s Quality First Award on March 29, 2023, for helping someone get back to work with a spare computer after theirs failed.


### The beginning of automation

I began automating within the provisioning role in 2023. The process work came first; automation followed the parts that had become stable and repeatable.
