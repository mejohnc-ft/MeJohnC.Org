For my birthday in 2011, I got a Toshiba Satellite laptop. But I wanted an iPad 2!

My parents thought the money would be better spent on a laptop. The Toshiba was a 17.3-inch P775D-S7230 running Windows 7 Home Premium. It was the machine I would later compare with my little 11-inch MacBook Air—and the Air would completely change what I expected from a computer.

<details><summary>The Toshiba’s specifications</summary>

- **Model:** Satellite P775D-S7230
- **Processor:** AMD A6-3400M
- **Memory:** 6GB DDR3 1333
- **Storage:** 640GB hard drive
- **Graphics:** AMD Radeon HD 6520G
- **Display:** 17.3-inch, 1600 × 900
- **Operating system:** Windows 7 Home Premium 64-bit

</details>

## But I wanted the iPad 2

That was the device I had my heart set on. Looking back, it helps explain why I was drawn to the Transformer Prime the next year: I was still looking for the tablet experience I wanted from the iPad.
