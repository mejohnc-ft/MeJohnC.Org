## Work

**2026 · So far**

This year, I’m proud of building tools around the people who use them: the service desk, the people looking after clients, and the team deciding what to automate next.

### Tools for everyday work

- [Service Toolbox](/projects/service-toolbox/): ticket context, guided automations, and investigations in one workspace.
- [Client Toolbox](/projects/client-toolbox/): a client book with evidence behind the scores and gaps that stay visible.
- [Vantage](/projects/vantage/): a place to turn feedback into scoped work with an owner.

Each project includes an interactive replica with fictional data. The demonstrations show selected workflows; the project descriptions distinguish my role from the team’s work.

### The next step for provisioning

**In progress · Third-party logistics (3PL)**

I’m investing in the next stage of the provisioning operation and preparing for a 3PL implementation. The implementation is ahead of us; it is not a completed result.

### Sharing the process

Our team’s process starts with service-desk feedback, gathers ideas through Vantage, and turns repeatable work into department-owned toolboxes. I contribute to direction, workflows, interfaces, implementation, and adoption.

- **September 4 · Rewst case study.** [Our team’s automation process](https://rewst.io/success-stories/how-centrexit-recovered-160-hours-a-month-using-rewst), reporting **160+ hours recovered each month** and the beginnings of automation as a service. This is a team outcome.
- **August 20 · Automation adoption webinar.** [The team’s playbook](https://rewst.io/resources/webinar/stop-waiting-start-now-scale-fast-centrexits-playbook): measurement, technician adoption, and reusable workflows.
- **June 25 · FLOW, Nashville.** I presented the service-delivery workspace and AI-assisted incident investigation alongside the automation team at [Community Live Open Mic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EInZA_rqaYE). The trip also left me with photographs of the city’s architecture and street art.
- **May 13 · Community contribution.** [M365 license cost reporting](https://youtu.be/458APcbS9aY): a multi-tenant workflow combining license inventory, cost validation, and usage into a client-ready report.
